# Garment surface photographs

The jackets, trousers and shoes use derivatives of two CC0 surfaces from Poly Haven.

| Source | Credits | Files used |
|---|---|---|
| [Brown Leather](https://polyhaven.com/a/brown_leather) | Rob Tuytel | Diffuse, OpenGL normals, roughness |
| [Rough Linen](https://polyhaven.com/a/rough_linen) | colormass and Rico Cilliers | Diffuse, OpenGL normals, roughness |

The photographs were resized and the diffuse images converted to neutral tones so the garments can be dyed individually. The garment builder combines these images with pattern-specific folds and topstitching and packs the finished maps into the character model. [Poly Haven asset license](https://polyhaven.com/license)
